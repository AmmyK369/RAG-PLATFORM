Amara Tech | Workplace and Office Rules
Document ID: OPS-RULE-008 | Version: 1.4 | Effective: 15 September 2026
Access: employee, engineer, admin

Amara Tech's registered office is at 4th Floor, Meridian Plaza, Sector 67, Mohali, Punjab. Standard office hours are 9:30 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., Monday through Friday, with a 45-minute lunch break. Employees may use the flexible arrival window from 8:30 to 10:30 a.m. if they complete 8 hours and 15 minutes of work and coordinate recurring schedules with their manager. The reception desk is staffed by Nitin Bedi from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Visitors must sign the digital visitor register, wear a visitor badge, and remain with their host. Meeting rooms are booked through RoomGrid; bookings are limited to 90 minutes when another team is waiting.

All staff must keep shared desks and meeting rooms clear at the end of the day. Food is permitted in the cafeteria on Level 4; drinks with secure lids are permitted at desks. Smoking and vaping are prohibited inside the building and within 10 metres of the main entrance. Employees should report damaged equipment, safety hazards, or access-card problems to Facilities at facilities@amaratech.example. Engineers working in the device lab must use the marked ESD mats and return test equipment to cabinet E-12 after use. Admin staff maintain the stationery inventory and should log purchases above ₹5,000 for approval by Finance Manager Devika Sood. The office emergency assembly point is the north parking area beside Gate B. These rules apply equally to employees, engineers, and administrators; the Operations Director, Arjun Mehta, owns this document.
