Amara Tech | Board Meeting Notes — Q3 FY2026
Document ID: GOV-BRD-2026-09 | Meeting date: 30 September 2026
Access: admin only | Strictly confidential

The board met at Amara Tech's Mohali office from 2:00 p.m. to 4:15 p.m. Attendees were Chairperson Leela Sethi, CEO Ananya Kapoor, CFO Rohit Bansal, and independent director Vikram Das. Company Secretary Pooja Arora recorded the minutes. Management reported Q3 revenue of ₹18.4 crore, up 12% quarter over quarter, and an operating margin of 14.2%. The board approved a maximum investment of ₹2.5 crore for the Atlas data-platform programme, released in two tranches: ₹1.5 crore after architecture review and ₹1 crore after the security-readiness assessment. CTO Sameer Khanna will present the architecture review by 15 November 2026. The board also noted that the largest three customers represented 31% of quarterly revenue.

The directors requested a hiring plan that limits net new headcount to 24 roles through March 2027, prioritizing platform reliability, applied AI, and enterprise sales. CFO Rohit Bansal will submit a revised cash-flow forecast by 12 October. The board deferred a proposed Bengaluru office lease until management provides a 3-year occupancy and cost comparison; no lease commitment is authorized. The directors discussed a potential strategic partnership with Northstar Systems, but instructed management not to disclose negotiation details outside the executive group until a term sheet is approved. The next board meeting is scheduled for 16 December 2026 at 10:00 a.m. IST.
