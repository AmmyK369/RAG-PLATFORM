Amara Tech | Information Security Policy
Document ID: SEC-POL-006 | Version: 2.2 | Effective: 20 October 2026
Access: engineer, admin

Amara Tech classifies information as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted. Source code, architecture diagrams, and internal operational metrics are Internal unless a project owner assigns a higher classification. Customer records, production credentials, private keys, and unreleased acquisition or financial information are Restricted. Restricted data may be accessed only by named, approved users with a documented business need. Access requests must be approved by the relevant system owner and Security Lead Priya Menon. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source control, cloud consoles, and production systems. Passwords must be at least 14 characters; shared accounts are prohibited. Secrets must be stored in the company vault, never in source code, chat messages, or local configuration committed to version control.

Company-managed laptops must run endpoint protection and receive critical security updates within 7 calendar days of release. Engineers must use synthetic or masked data in development and testing; copying production customer data to a personal device is prohibited. Suspected phishing, lost devices, exposed credentials, or accidental data disclosure must be reported to security@amaratech.example within 30 minutes of discovery. Security will triage high-severity reports within 1 hour and may temporarily suspend affected accounts while investigating. Administrators must review privileged access every quarter and remove access within 4 hours of a confirmed termination notice. Audit logs are retained for 365 days. Exceptions require a written risk assessment, a named approver, and an expiry date not exceeding 90 days. This policy is owned by Chief Information Security Officer Priya Menon.
