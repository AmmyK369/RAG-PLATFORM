The following annual base-pay bands are planning ranges in Indian rupees and are not individual salary commitments. Software Engineer I: ₹6,00,000–₹9,00,000; Software Engineer II: ₹9,00,000–₹14,00,000; Senior Software Engineer: ₹14,00,000–₹22,00,000; Engineering Manager: ₹22,00,000–₹32,00,000; People Operations Associate: ₹4,50,000–₹7,00,000; Office Administrator: ₹3,60,000–₹5,40,000. Offers within a band depend on role scope, relevant experience, interview evidence, internal equity, and approved headcount. Variable pay, benefits, joining incentives, and equity awards are excluded from these base-pay figures. Finance must approve any offer above the midpoint of its band, and CEO approval is required for an offer above the band maximum.

The annual compensation review begins each February, with manager recommendations due by 15 February and calibration completed by 10 March. The review panel consists of CEO Ananya Kapoor, CFO Rohit Bansal, and Chief People Officer Kavita Rao. The standard planning pool for merit increases is 7% of eligible annual base payroll; this is a company planning assumption and may be revised by the board. Promotions require evidence that the employee has performed the next-level responsibilities for at least 3 months and must be supported by a revised role description. Administrators may access this document only where required for approved compensation, payroll, or audit work. It must not be shared with employees or engineers without written authorization from the Chief People Officer. The document owner is Kavita Rao.
