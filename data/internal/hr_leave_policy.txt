Amara Tech | Human Resources Leave Policy
Document ID: HR-POL-014 | Version: 2.1 | Effective: 1 October 2026
Access: employee, engineer, admin

Amara Tech provides paid time off to support employee wellbeing while maintaining reliable team coverage. Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual earned leave and 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at 1.5 days per completed month; unused earned leave may be carried forward up to a maximum balance of 30 days. Sick leave does not carry forward. Employees should submit planned leave in the PeopleDesk portal at least 5 working days before the first day away. Their reporting manager must approve the request before travel is booked. In an emergency, the employee must notify the manager and People Operations by 10:00 a.m. on the first day of absence. The People Operations contact is Rhea Malhotra (people@amaratech.example).

For leave longer than 5 consecutive working days, the manager must confirm a coverage plan and identify an acting contact. Engineers must record open incidents, deployment commitments, and code-review handoffs in the team tracker before leave begins. Managers should respond to routine requests within 2 working days; requests are escalated to People Operations if no decision is recorded. Parental leave and statutory holidays are administered under the applicable local law and the employee's contract, not deducted from earned leave. This policy applies to full-time employees, including engineers and administrators. Contractors should follow the leave terms in their individual agreements. The policy owner is Chief People Officer Kavita Rao; questions or exceptions require written approval from People Operations.
